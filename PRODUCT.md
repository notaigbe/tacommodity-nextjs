# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Primary: partners and buyers of smallholder produce, such as off-takers, processors and development agencies (Okomu Oil Palm Company, PIND, IDH). They visit to judge whether TA Commodity is a credible, established and dependable supply partner. Secondary audiences are smallholder farmers and cooperatives, investors, and job seekers (the TA Commodity careers pages).

## Product Purpose
TA Commodity Producers Co Nig Ltd is a Nigerian social-impact agricultural company that organises smallholder farmers into reliable supply networks, for example Fresh Fruit Bunch supply to Okomu. The site also hosts subsidiary brands (PHAGRO / Women Sustainable Group, Friends of Smart Plantation Growers Initiative, BFG Capital Management). Success means a partner or buyer leaves convinced that TA Commodity delivers at scale and keeps delivering.

## Brand Personality
Grounded, proven, partnered. The voice is rooted in fieldwork, backed by real figures (tonnage, years, farmers reached) and focused on long-term relationships. It is confident without hype, and specific rather than aspirational.

## Anti-references
- Generic NGO charity sites: pity imagery, donation-appeal tone.
- Flashy SaaS / startup pages: gradients, glass cards, hype metrics.
- Dated corporate Bootstrap business templates.
- Overly busy or animated pages: motion everywhere, scrolling tickers, distractions.

## Design Principles
1. Proof over promise: lead with real figures, named partners and field imagery instead of adjectives.
2. Let partners speak through association: present partner logos with respect and calm, never as filler decoration.
3. Quiet confidence: motion happens once and supports reading; nothing loops for attention.
4. Rooted, not rustic: use field and plantation imagery, finished with a professional trade-house standard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body text contrast of at least 4.5:1 and meaningful alt text on partner logos and field imagery. Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion. Many visitors are on mobile over variable Nigerian network conditions, so keep pages lightweight and readable at phone width.
